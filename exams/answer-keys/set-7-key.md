# ANSWER KEY — Set 7  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Delivered but missing / possible theft.**
- **Route:** Marked delivered / lost. **Action:** Empathize; confirm the shipping address and the carrier's delivered-scan. Advise checking around the property/with household, and that a **carrier claim/investigation** can be opened for a package scanned delivered but not received. Escalate for the reship-or-refund decision per policy.
- **Trap:** promising an instant free reship/refund before verifying the scan/address. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — CS.COM processing time.**
- **Route:** Processing time (CS.COM). **Action:** Confirm "Awaiting Fulfillment"; quote **3–4 business days** processing for capellisport.com orders, then **1–3 business days** USPS standard shipping. No expedite. Tracking email on ship.
- **Trap:** quoting the ~5-week team-kit timeline for a CS.COM order, or promising an unconfirmed date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Wrong item shipped (color/type).**
- **Route:** Wrong item received. **Action:** **Capelli's error** (ordered red keeper shirts, sent blue field shirts). Request an **evidence photo** of the wrong items, then ship the **correct items**. Quality opener.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** treating it as a customer return, or replacing with no photo. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Personalized item, but OUR error.**
- **Route:** Damaged/Defective / wrong item (customized). **Action:** The non-returnable rule for personalized items **does not apply when Capelli made the error** — a misspelled name we printed is our fault. Request a **photo** showing the misspelling, then arrange a **same-size replacement** with the correct spelling.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** hiding behind "personalized items are non-returnable" and refusing — it's our error; or shipping a replacement with no photo. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Partial (single-item) cancellation.**
- **Route:** Order cancellation (item). **Action:** Confirm the socks aren't already fulfilled; cancel just that item and **refund its value** to the original payment (3–5 business days as "CAPELLI SPORT"); the rest of the order ships normally, tracking email to follow.
- **Trap:** cancelling/refunding the whole order, or refunding before verifying fulfillment. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Applying patches.**
- **Route:** Ordering help / product (patches). **Action:** A standard home iron is **not recommended**; ideally an industrial heat press. If ironing at home, place a cloth/plastic between the logo and the iron and test first (apply, wash, check adherence). Heat press guidance: ~**315–320°F for 10–15 seconds** with medium pressure to set, then turn the garment inside out and press ~**20–25 seconds** from the back.
- **Trap:** telling them to just iron directly on the patch with no protection/testing. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — International order.**
- **Route:** International shipping. **Action:** Provide the club shop link; they add their address/country at checkout. Note they are **responsible for any customs/duty fees** and must have a **PayPal account** to complete an international order.
- **Trap:** omitting the PayPal requirement or the customs/duties responsibility. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — OOS cancel-or-wait.**
- **Route:** Out of stock. **Action:** Confirm OOS; offer to **cancel + refund** or **wait** for restock (or an alternative if available). Ask them to advise how to proceed.
- **Trap:** promising a firm restock date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Compensation / goodwill request.**
- **Route:** Escalation. **Action:** Acknowledge the specific problems (not a generic apology). Goodwill (coupon/refund/replacement) is **not automatic** — it's a **manager-approved exception**; escalate rather than promising compensation yourself. (E.g., a one-time discount coupon may be approved.)
- **Trap:** promising or issuing compensation on your own authority. *(4/3/3)*

**Q10 — Multi-part policy.** ≈3.3 each:
1. **Military discount:** Yes — **10% off the first order**; request proof (military health/insurance card, Veterans ID, or veteran designation on license) before applying/refunding.
2. **Promo code:** We are **not currently offering discounts/promotions**; invite them to **sign up for the newsletter** to hear about future ones.
3. **Gift certificate:** Redeemable for club items at **teams.capellisport.com** (and Capelli equipment/fitness via the provided links) using the gift code.
- **Trap:** inventing a promo code, or applying the military discount without proof. *(10)*
