# ANSWER KEY — Set 4  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Partial / held shipment.**
- **Route:** Partial shipment. **Action:** Check the order — often held because an added piece delayed it. Find the tracking + ETA in the shipment inbox and reply with which pieces have shipped and which are pending, using the partial-shipment tracking template.
- **Trap:** quoting an ETA not confirmed in the system. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — Referee club processing time.**
- **Route:** Processing time (referee). **Action:** Confirm "Awaiting Fulfillment"; quote **5–10 business days** processing for referee clubs (know the variants: majority team kit ~5 weeks, CS.com 3–4 business days, Real Coast ~2 weeks). No expedite; 1–3 business days shipping after.
- **Trap:** quoting the wrong club's timeline, or promising an unconfirmed ETA. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Defective non-decoration item (windbreaker).**
- **Route:** Damaged/Defective. **Action:** Yes — defective non-decoration items qualify. Request a **clear evidence photo** first, then replace in the **same size/form**. Use the quality opener.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** replacing with no photo, or a different size. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — General return.**
- **Route:** Return/Exchange. **Action:** **No exchange** — return for refund. Within **30 days**, **unworn/unwashed, tags attached**; personalized items non-returnable; **shipping costs not refunded**. Include name/address/order#/reason in the package. Ship to **Capelli Sport Returns, 901 Sathers Drive, Pittston, PA 18640**. They may place a new order in the right size anytime.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** offering a direct exchange. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Never received, season over.**
- **Route:** Lost shipment → **Escalation.** **Action:** Do **not** default to a reship when the need has passed. Acknowledge the missed prior contacts, take ownership, and **escalate for a refund/credit decision.** Don't loop generic holding replies.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** auto-reshipping (or stalling with another holding reply) instead of escalating for a refund. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Add items to existing order.**
- **Route:** Add items. **Action:** **We cannot add to an existing order.** They must place a **new order**; as a **one-time courtesy**, ask them to reply with the new order # so shipping can be refunded.
- **Trap:** saying you'll add the socks to the existing order. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Player link in database.**
- **Route:** Player link (in DB). **Action:** Since the roster entry is in our system, provide the **player link** for the child so they can order. (If it were *not* in the DB, you'd direct them to the coach/club admin.)
- **Trap:** refusing when we actually have it, or sharing another player's link. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — Out of stock options.**
- **Route:** Out of stock. **Action:** No firm restock date; offer: **wait** for restock, **alternative size/color** if available, or **cancel + refund**. Email Planning to replenish; advise once restocked.
- **Trap:** promising a firm restock date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Customer wants the RO number.**
- **Route:** (Replacement/tracking). **Action:** **Never share the internal RO (replacement order) number.** Reassure that a replacement has been arranged and that you'll send the **tracking number** once it ships — that's what they use to track.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** giving the customer the RO#. *(4/3/3)*

**Q10 — Shipping-refund goodwill.**
- **Route:** Refund request (goodwill). **Action:** A shipping-cost refund for excessive delay is a **goodwill gesture that requires internal approval** — don't promise it outright. Acknowledge the specific delay, escalate for approval, and once approved advise ~24h to process (3–5 business days to appear). Own the follow-up.
- **Trap:** promising the shipping refund before it's approved. *(4/3/3)*
