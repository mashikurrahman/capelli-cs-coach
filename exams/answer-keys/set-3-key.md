# ANSWER KEY — Set 3  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Returned to sender.**
- **Route:** Returned to sender. **Action:** Advise the fulfillment center returned the order (e.g., insufficient/incorrect address). **Confirm the correct shipping address on file** with the customer, then reship once confirmed.
- **Trap:** reshipping to the same unverified address, or refunding without offering the reship. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — Pre-order vs stock timeline confusion.**
- **Route:** Out of stock / processing. **Action:** Acknowledge the confusion empathetically. Explain the timelines: **in-stock items 3–7 business days**, **pre-orders 3–6 weeks**, **mandatory/team kits ~5 weeks**. No expedite. Confirm which applies to their item.
- **Trap:** offering to speed it up / promising the original 3–7 days. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Wrong-club decoration, whole team.**
- **Route:** Wrong item received (production error, team-wide). **Action:** Request **one clear photo of ALL affected items** in a single shot; **escalate** to operations/manager — this is a production/artwork error that can repeat. Do **not** blind-reship.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** reshipping without escalation (the wrong artwork just repeats). *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Return one mandatory-kit item.**
- **Route:** Return/Exchange (mandatory-kit rule). **Action:** Items from the mandatory package are **not eligible for return on their own.** However, if the customer places a **new order with the correct size**, we'll refund the returned item(s) once we receive them. No exchanges. 30-day window, unworn/unwashed, tags on.
- **Trap:** accepting the single item as a standalone return/exchange. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Shopify cancellation.**
- **Route:** Order cancellation (Shopify/FBPA). **Action:** Confirm it isn't fulfilled/shipped; cancel the order on Shopify and refund to the original payment. For Shopify, quote **up to two weeks** for the refund to reflect as "CAPELLI SPORT" (note: BigCommerce cancellations quote 3–5 business days — quote the right one for the platform). They may reorder anytime.
- **Trap:** quoting the wrong platform's refund timing, or cancelling an already-fulfilled order. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Two players, one account.**
- **Route:** Ordering help (two players). **Action:** After signing in, select the kit from the left menu, choose the **first** player's team + name, add items, then **"Proceed to Cart"** (this only adds to cart, not checkout). Then select the kit again for the **second** player, change the team/name, add items, and check out from the cart at the top.
- **Trap:** telling them to place two fully separate orders when one cart works. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Player number change.**
- **Route:** Order change / adjustment. **Action:** We **cannot change the number without the coach's confirmation.** First verify the order is **not yet in production**; if not, put the order **on hold** (email Operations) and ask the customer to **CC their coach** to confirm the correct number. Numbers/roster are club-controlled.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** changing the number instantly without coach confirmation / production check. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — Discontinued item.**
- **Route:** Out of stock (no restock). **Action:** Advise the item is out of stock and **will not be replenished**; recommend exploring similar available products; apologize for the inconvenience.
- **Trap:** implying it might come back / promising a restock. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Spot the mistake.** Full credit = all **four** internal leaks caught (2.5 each):
1. **Names an internal contact** ("emailed Dan") — never surface internal names.
2. **Reveals internal approval chain** ("awaiting BD's approval") — internal only.
3. **Includes a do-not-respond instruction** ("PLS DO NOT RESPOND") — that's an internal note, never sent to a customer.
4. **Leaks an internal Wave number** ("Wave #77") — internal fulfillment reference.
- **Corrected message:** a clean customer-facing note — e.g., acknowledge the issue, say the appropriate department is working on the replacement, and that you'll follow up with an update — with **none** of the internal details, and set Open + a separate internal note. *(10)*

**Q10 — Repeat defect.**
- **Route:** Damaged/Defective → **Escalation.** **Action:** A replacement that came back with the **same defect** is a red flag — do **not** re-issue the same order blindly. Request a photo, **escalate to a manager**, and confirm the **corrected artwork/decoration** before reshipping. Acknowledge it's the second time specifically.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** firing off another identical replacement (the loop that caused the complaint). *(4/3/3)*
