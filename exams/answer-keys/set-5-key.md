# ANSWER KEY — Set 5  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Mixed / two-shipment order.**
- **Route:** Shipping (mixed order). **Action:** Reassure nothing is wrong — the order ships in **two separate shipments**, each with its own tracking email. The first part ships shortly and the second follows. Offer to help if anything's missing after both arrive.
- **Trap:** treating it as a duplicate/error and cancelling or "fixing" it. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — ETA, no answer yet.**
- **Route:** ETA / order status. **Action:** Empathize and apologize for the delay; tell them you're **checking with the fulfillment center** and will provide an update on their order within **2–3 business days**. Don't invent a date.
- **Trap:** promising an ETA/date that isn't confirmed. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Plain/blank item (wrong item).**
- **Route:** Wrong item received. **Action:** Capelli error — request a **clear evidence photo** of the blank/wrong item (showing size tag) before acting; then arrange the correct item. Shopify order = FBPA. Use the quality opener.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** promising a refund/replacement with no photo. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Youth instead of adult (customer error).**
- **Route:** Return/Exchange. **Action:** **No exchanges** — this was the customer's ordering choice. Send the return policy; they return for a refund and place a new order in the correct size.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** processing a size/style exchange. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Cancel (credit not applied).**
- **Route:** Order cancellation. **Action:** Confirm the details (the missing credit/coupon), confirm the order is **not yet fulfilled**, then cancel on the platform and process the refund. Refund posts per platform (BC 3–5 business days as "CAPELLI SPORT"). They can reorder with the credit applied.
- **Trap:** cancelling/refunding before verifying it isn't already fulfilled. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Size help.**
- **Route:** Size help. **Action:** Point them to the **size chart**: click the item image, scroll to the last image in the gallery for the chart. Help them compare their child's measurements. (Store access is via the club password/app.)
- **Trap:** guessing a size / promising a fit instead of directing to the chart. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Store access.**
- **Route:** Team-store access (club-managed). **Action:** Store access is **managed by the club** — direct the customer to their coach/club administrator. **Never share the club password.**
- **Trap (auto-fail):** sharing the club password. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — Restock request.**
- **Route:** Out of stock (replenish). **Action:** Tell them you've contacted the appropriate department (Planning) about replenishing the specific SKU/size, and you'll **update them once it's restocked**. Don't promise a firm date.
- **Trap:** promising a specific restock date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Legal / complaint escalation.**
- **Route:** Escalation. **Action:** Stay calm and professional; acknowledge the specific issue (not a generic apology). **Escalate** to a manager; goodwill (replacement/coupon/refund) may be approved as an exception. Email only — no phone number handed out. Open + internal note.
- **Trap:** making a promise/threat-driven concession on the spot, or giving out an internal contact. *(4/3/3)*

**Q10 — Spot the mistake.** Full credit = all **three** caught (≈3.3 each):
1. **Leaks the club password** ("SOCA26") — never share a club password; store access is club-managed.
2. **Promises expedited/overnight shipping** — we do **not** offer expedited shipping.
3. **Promises arrival for the game** — never promise a delivery date/event arrival.
- **Corrected handling:** direct them to their coach/club admin for store access; explain no expedited shipping and quote the standard timeline; no date promised.
- Scoring: leaking the password alone = **automatic fail** on this question regardless of the rest. *(10)*
