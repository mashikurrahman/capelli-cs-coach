# ANSWER KEY — Set 1  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Label Created / not moving.**
- **Route:** Tracking / order-status. **Action:** Reassure — the shipping label is generated *before* the parcel physically ships; UPS shows no movement until it scans the package. Tell them UPS is expected to receive it by end of week and to reach back if there's no update in **2–3 business days**. Provide the UPS tracking link.
- **Must NOT:** promise a hard delivery date; offer to expedite.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** promising a specific delivery date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — Expedite request.**
- **Route:** Expedite/processing-time. **Action:** Politely explain **we do not offer expedited shipping**; orders are worked in the order received. Quote the correct processing timeline and confirm the order is awaiting fulfillment. Empathize with the deadline but do **not** promise arrival by the event.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** promising it will arrive in time / offering rush shipping. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Number peeling (decoration defect).**
- **Route:** Damaged/Defective. **Action:** Request a **clear evidence photo** (showing the size tag) *before* acting. Use the quality-checks opener. Then replace in the **same size** originally ordered.
- **Policy:** replacements are same-size/same-form only; evidence photo required first.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** shipping a replacement with no photo, or in a different size. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Too small, wants exchange.**
- **Route:** Return/Exchange. **Action:** **No exchanges.** Customer returns for a refund (30 days, unworn/unwashed, tags on) and places a new order in the desired size. Give the return address (Capelli Sport Returns, 901 Sathers Drive, Pittston, PA 18640). Note shipping costs aren't refunded; personalized items non-returnable.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** offering a direct size exchange. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Duplicate order, cancel one.**
- **Route:** Order cancellation. **Action:** Confirm the order is **not yet fulfilled** (check it isn't in the FBB list / not shipped or delivered in SAP). If not fulfilled, cancel on BigCommerce and process the refund through the refunds team; refund posts in **3–5 business days** as "CAPELLI SPORT". If already processing/shipped, it cannot be cancelled.
- **Trap:** cancelling/refunding before verifying fulfillment status; naming the internal refunds team to the customer. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Individual items without the whole kit.**
- **Route:** Individual item ordering. **Action:** 24h after the mandatory kit is purchased, sign in to teams.us.capellisport.com with the **same email**, on a **PC/Mac**; select the club + club password; choose the kit/player; then click **"Enable Adding Individual Products"** to add single items to cart. Cannot place a new order directly from a previous order.
- **Trap:** saying you can add the socks to their existing order (you can't). *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Team store password.**
- **Route:** Team-store access (club-managed). **Action:** Team-store access is managed by the **club** — direct the customer to their coach/club administrator. **Never share a club password.**
- **Trap (auto-fail):** giving the customer any club password. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — Out of stock.**
- **Route:** Out of stock. **Action:** Confirm the item/size is OOS with no firm restock date; offer options: **cancel + refund**, **wait** for restock, or an **alternative size/color** if available. Email Planning to replenish if appropriate; advise once restocked.
- **Trap:** promising a firm restock date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Manager / review escalation.**
- **Route:** Escalation. **Action:** **Phone support is not offered — email only**; do not hand out a manager's phone number. Acknowledge the specific problem (not a generic apology), and escalate the complaint/review threat to a manager, where goodwill may be approved as an exception. Set Open + internal note.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** giving out an internal phone number or contact. *(4/3/3)*

**Q10 — Spot the mistake.** Full credit requires catching **all four**:
1. **Offers an exchange** ("we'll exchange your medium for a large") — no exchanges; must be return-for-refund + new order, OR if it's *our* error, a same-size replacement (not an upsize).
2. **Promises a hard delivery date / guarantee** ("arrive by Friday guaranteed") — never promise a date.
3. **Leaks the internal RO number** ("RO#48213") — RO numbers must never go to a customer.
4. **Ships a different size** as the "replacement" — replacements are same-size only.
- **Corrected handling:** if customer-fault size → return-for-refund + new order; if Capelli error → request evidence photo, then same-size replacement, no date promised, no RO# shared.
- Scoring: 2.5 pts per error identified. Miss the RO# leak → cap the question at 5. *(10)*
