# ANSWER KEY — Set 2  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Marked delivered, not received.**
- **Route:** Marked-delivered / lost. **Action:** Empathize; verify the shipping address on file and the carrier's delivery scan. Advise checking with neighbors/household and around the property, and (for USPS/UPS) that a carrier claim/investigation may be opened. Escalate for a reship-or-refund decision per policy rather than promising one instantly.
- **Trap:** promising an immediate free reship or refund before verifying the delivery scan/address. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — Order status, team kit.**
- **Route:** Order status / processing time. **Action:** Confirm the order is "Awaiting Fulfillment." Quote the **~5-week** processing time for mandatory team kits; state we do **not** offer expedited shipping and orders are worked in the order received; they'll get a tracking email when it ships (allow 1–3 business days shipping after).
- **Trap:** promising an ETA not confirmed from the system, or quoting the wrong timeline. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — We shipped wrong size (Capelli error).**
- **Route:** Wrong item received. **Action:** This is **Capelli's error** (order says Large, we sent Medium). Request an **evidence photo** of the wrong item (showing size tag), then ship a **replacement in the correct/ordered size** (Large). Use the quality/apology opener.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** treating it as a customer-fault return, or replacing with no photo. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Personalized/customized item return.**
- **Route:** Customized item return. **Action:** **Personalized products (name/number) are not returnable or exchangeable** — unless Capelli made an error (not the case here). Politely explain the personalization policy. No exchange.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** accepting a personalized item as a normal return, or offering an exchange. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Refund status follow-up.**
- **Route:** Refund request (status). **Action:** Refunds take ~24h to process and **3–5 business days** to appear, showing as **"CAPELLI SPORT"**. If it's past that window, tell them you're checking with the refunds team and will follow up — do **not** promise a new date until confirmed. Own the follow-up.
- **Trap:** inventing a new refund date; naming the internal refunds team (ROLO) to the customer. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Phone checkout / cart button.**
- **Route:** Website issue. **Action:** On a phone held **vertically**, the CART button doesn't show; turn the phone **horizontal** and scroll to the top to see CART + item count, then tap it to check out. Offer further help if still stuck. (Also: mandatory-kit items must stay in the cart — removing a required item breaks checkout.)
- **Trap:** telling them the site is broken / escalating instead of giving the known fix. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Player link (needs own).**
- **Route:** Player link (club-managed). **Action:** Player links are managed by the **club** — we can't create them from outside. Direct the customer to their coach/club administrator to have the child's name/email added to the correct roster; then we can help. If the link is already in our database, provide it.
- **Trap:** promising to generate a link yourself. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — OOS, needs alternative.**
- **Route:** Out of stock. **Action:** Confirm OOS with no firm restock date; offer an **alternative size or color** if available, or **cancel + refund**, or **wait**. Let them choose. Can email Planning to replenish and advise once restocked.
- **Trap:** promising a firm restock date, or shipping an alternative without confirming the customer wants it. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Complimentary hoodie charged.**
- **Route:** Escalation. **Action:** This is a **known club-wide billing issue** — escalate to the internal team/manager; reply that the correct department is engaged; set **Open + internal note**. Do not refund on the spot without the escalation.
- **Trap:** naming the internal contact (Dan) to the customer, or promising the refund before escalation. *(4/3/3)*

**Q10 — Multi-issue.** Full credit = all three routed correctly (≈3.3 each):
1. **No confirmation / order status** → check the order in the system; if placed, confirm status + processing timeline; if truly no order exists, advise it may not have gone through.
2. **Can't access Lightning FC store** → club-managed access; direct to coach/club admin; never share a password.
3. **Add a keeper jersey** → **cannot add to an existing order**; place a **new order**; as a one-time courtesy, reply with the new order # so shipping can be refunded.
- **Trap:** merging these into one wrong resolution, or offering to add the jersey to the existing order. *(10)*
