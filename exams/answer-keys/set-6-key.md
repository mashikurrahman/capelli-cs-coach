# ANSWER KEY — Set 6  *(grader only)*

Rubric per question: **Route/action (4) · Policy stated (3) · Trap avoided (3)** unless noted.

---

**Q1 — Shipped but tracking shows no info.**
- **Route:** Tracking / order status. **Action:** Explain the order has **departed our warehouse and is in transit to UPS** for final delivery; the tracking number is generated but **won't display movement until UPS scans it**. Provide the UPS link; if no update within **2–3 business days** after the expected hand-off, contact us.
- **Trap:** promising a hard delivery date, or saying it's lost. *(4/3/3)*

**Q2 — Rush + shipping refund.**
- **Route:** Expedite → Refund (goodwill). **Action:** Two parts: (1) we do **not** offer expedited shipping — explain and quote the timeline; (2) a shipping-cost refund for the delay is a **goodwill gesture needing internal approval** — don't promise it outright; escalate, and if approved advise the timing.
- **Trap:** promising either the rush or the shipping refund on the spot. *(4/3/3)*

**Q3 — Bag/equipment defect.**
- **Route:** Damaged/Defective (equipment). **Action:** Request **photos** of the damage; use the quality opener. A bag/equipment replacement may require **internal (DM) approval** before the replacement order is created. Replace same item/size.
- **Trap (auto-fail):** promising the replacement with no photo; or naming the internal approver (Dan/DM) to the customer. *(4/3/3)*

**Q4 — Shipping refund on a return.**
- **Route:** Return/Exchange. **Action:** Explain that **return shipping costs are not refunded** — Capelli is not responsible for refunding shipping costs. Refund is for the item's value once received (within policy).
- **Trap:** telling them shipping will be refunded on a standard return. *(4/3/3)*

**Q5 — Goodwill shipping refund (delay).**
- **Route:** Refund request (goodwill). **Action:** For an **excessive delay**, a shipping-cost refund is a goodwill gesture **requiring internal approval**. Acknowledge the specific delay; escalate; once approved advise ~24h to process (3–5 business days to appear). Own the follow-up rather than looping holds.
- **Trap:** promising the refund before approval. *(4/3/3)*

**Q6 — Guest checkout, can't see order / order individual.**
- **Route:** Account & login (guest). **Action:** Explain that no account exists yet, so they can't view order status or order individual items. Ask them to **create an account** at the create-account link using the **same email** as the order; once created, we can **link the order** so they can order individual items. Confirm back when done.
- **Trap:** promising individual-item ordering without the account/link step. *(4/3/3)*

**Q7 — Number shows old (club-controlled).**
- **Route:** Player link / order change (roster). **Action:** Player **numbers/roster are entered by the club admin**, not Capelli — we can't change it on our side. Direct the customer (ideally CC the club) to have the **club update the roster**; then order with the correct number.
- **Trap:** promising to change the number yourself. *(4/3/3)*

**Q8 — OOS, flexible on color.**
- **Route:** Out of stock (alt color). **Action:** Confirm the size is OOS with no firm restock date; offer the **same item/size in an alternative color** if available (or wait / cancel+refund). Confirm before shipping an alternative.
- **Trap:** shipping an alternative without confirmation, or promising a restock date. *(4/3/3)*

**Q9 — Spot the mistake.** Full credit = all caught (≈2.5 each):
1. **Names internal contacts** ("emailed GT and RI in planning") — never surface internal names/teams.
2. **Leaks internal system term** ("OBD status") — internal fulfillment reference.
3. **Leaks internal process** ("once the wave releases") — internal.
4. **Internal instruction sent to customer** ("please don't reply") — that's an internal note, not customer-facing.
- **Corrected message:** acknowledge you're checking on the order status with the fulfillment center and will update them within 2–3 business days — none of the internal detail; keep internal notes internal. *(10)*

**Q10 — Missing item → refund.**
- **Route:** Missing item → Refund request. **Action:** Confirm the missing line item (white hat) against the order. Normally we ship the missing item; since the customer now prefers a **refund**, process the refund for that item (per policy/approval) rather than reshipping. Refund posts in 3–5 business days as "CAPELLI SPORT."
- **Trap:** ignoring the refund request and reshipping anyway, or refunding without confirming the item was actually missing. *(4/3/3)*
