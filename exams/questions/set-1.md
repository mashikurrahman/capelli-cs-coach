**Q1.** A customer writes: *"I got the email saying my order shipped 4 days ago, but UPS still just says 'Label Created' and it hasn't moved. Did it actually ship?"*

**Q3.** *"My daughter wore her jersey twice — washed cold, hung dry — and the number is already peeling off."*

**Q4.** *"The shirt we got is too small. Can I just exchange it for the next size up?"*

**Q5.** *"I accidentally placed this order twice. Please cancel one of them — I only need one."*

**Q6.** *"I don't want to buy the whole mandatory kit again — I just need to order a couple of extra socks for my son. How?"*

**Q7.** *"I can't get into the Rush Maryland team store — what's the password?"*
How do you handle this, and what must you never do?

**Q8.** *"The jersey size I need is out of stock. When will it be back, and what are my options right now?"*

**Q9.** *"This is ridiculous. Give me a phone number for your manager — I'm going to leave a Trustpilot review."*
